# Scripture Source Evidence

Retrieved and reviewed: 2026-07-26

This manifest records the provenance and rights verification for Lumen's six
bundled Scripture editions. It is evidence for attribution and release checks,
not a substitute for legal review. The bundled Scripture files are immutable
under `docs/scripture-integrity.md`.

The verdicts below are enforced in code by `src/data/scriptureRights.ts` and
`npm run release-gate`. Verification was performed for roadmap item 10.

### Text drift against current upstream (open finding)

The bundled Turkish text was compared verse-by-verse against the 2026-07-26
download using the same parse as `scripts/build-bible.mjs`: 66/66 books,
**31,059 verses compared, 4 differences.** Structure and verse counts match
exactly. Lumen did not modify the text; upstream has since corrected these four
verses, so the bundle is a verbatim copy of an *earlier* YTC revision.

| Reference | Bundled | Current upstream |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 Kings 19:8 | "Aşur Kralı'nın Livna'ya karşı savaşır buldu" | "Aşur Kralı'nı Livna'ya karşı savaşır buldu" |
| 2 Kings 20:6 | "kulum David'in hatırına" | "hizmetkârım David'in hatırına" |
| 2 Kings 20:19 | "esenlik ve gerçek olacak" | "esenlik ve doğruluk olacak" |
| Acts 7:45 | "o ulusları Tanrı David'in günlerine" | "o ulusları Tanrı, David'in günlerine" |

This is a scripture-integrity matter rather than a licensing one: attribution and
license conditions are satisfied either way. Re-exporting the current upstream
revision is permitted by `docs/scripture-integrity.md` ("a newly verified verbatim
export of the same approved edition") but changes the bundled SHA-256, so it needs
an explicit decision by the product owner. Left open.

## Portuguese — João Ferreira de Almeida (revision unidentified)

**Verdict: NOT VERIFIED. Release-blocked.** Neither the edition nor its rights
status is established, so it may not be presented as public domain.

- Bundled file: `src/data/bible-full.pt.json`
- Bundled SHA-256: `f9c4527f7c009494bfdad0ca57a5d9588c98e87e29ff25336e8e9a8041033708`
- Imported source: https://github.com/seven1m/open-bibles/blob/master/por-almeida.usfx.xml
- Immutable source Git blob SHA: `3f7971040c33a77329b8c73f0918ae2f0db05155`
- Upstream manifest label: public domain (https://github.com/seven1m/open-bibles/blob/master/README.md)

### Why verification failed

Artifact inspection (2026-07-26) of `por-almeida.usfx.xml`:

- The file has **no `<header>`, no `<rights>` element, and no `<languageCode>`** —
  it begins directly at `<book id="GEN">`. It carries no rights statement at all.
- It names **no dated revision**. "João Ferreira de Almeida" alone spans editions
  from 1681 to modern revisions, some of which are actively under copyright (for
  example Almeida Revista e Atualizada, © Sociedade Bíblica do Brasil).
- Its wording does not match a single well-known revision cleanly. Genesis 1:2
  reads "A terra era sem forma e vazia; e havia trevas sobre a face do abismo,
  mas o Espírito de Deus pairava sobre a face das águas" — the "mas … pairava"
  phrasing matches neither Revista e Corrigida nor Revista e Atualizada as
  commonly published. The text uses "Jeová" 28 times and mixed-case "Senhor"
  (Psalm 23:1: "O Senhor é o meu pastor; nada me faltará"), and pre-1990
  Brazilian orthography ("dêem").

The sole basis for the public-domain claim is the upstream repository's own
README label. Roadmap item 10 forbids presenting text of an unclear edition as
public domain, so this locale is blocked.

## French — Bible J.F. Ostervald 1996

**Verdict: NOT VERIFIED. Release-blocked.** An identifiable 1996 reviser and
publisher exist, and no public-domain dedication by them was found.

- Bundled file: `src/data/bible-full.fr.json`
- Bundled SHA-256: `2e6d48e7272828ffc736c2b196699e0429ba1d56152708e8dca32bac8f1e7e0c`
- Imported source: https://github.com/seven1m/open-bibles/blob/master/fra-ostervald.osis.xml
- Immutable source Git blob SHA: `cc262519cd03e4813f9f70e69681684ac66a3a94`
- Upstream manifest label: "French Ostervald 1996 — Public Domain"

### Why verification failed

1. **The "public domain" label is a third-party self-declaration, not the rights
   holder's.** The OSIS header of the imported file states
   `<rights>Public Domain</rights>` but attributes the packaging to
   `<publisher>FreeBibleSoftwareGroup</publisher>`, `<creator>TextFactory</creator>`,
   `<contributor>Louange.org</contributor>`, converted by `ZefToOsis 1.0.0` from
   `<source>http://unbound.biola.edu/…</source>`. None of these is the 1996
   reviser or publisher. Mirrors such as gratis.bible repeat this same header, so
   they are not independent corroboration.

2. **The 1996 revision contains 1996 editorial work.** Its own preface
   (https://github.com/bible-hub/Bibles/blob/master/French__Ostervald_(1996_revision)__ostervald__LTR-copyright.html)
   states the work is "une mise à jour de l'édition de 1886 d'une révision de la
   Bible d'Ostervald publiée pour la première fois en 1881" and lists the 1996
   editors' own changes: Roman numerals removed, text recut into verses, notes
   regrouped, spelling and grammar modernised, obsolete words replaced, and
   "quelques altérations … au texte de 1886 pour raison de fidélité au Texte
   Reçu" (including "antichrist" for "antechrist"). The preface carries no
   copyright notice and names no rights holder.

3. **The 1996 reviser and publisher are identifiable.** The revision was edited
   under Pastor C. H. Boughman and published in 1996 by Bearing Precious Seed,
   Milford, Ohio (https://levigilant.com/bible_ostervald/remarques.html;
   corroborated by https://www.wayoflife.org/reports/bearing_precious_seed.html).
   A 1996 US publication is protected without formalities, so absent a dedication
   from Boughman or Bearing Precious Seed the 1996 editorial layer is presumptively
   in copyright. No such dedication was found.

The underlying 1881 Frossard / Société Biblique de France revision and its 1886
edition **are** unambiguously public domain — only the 1996 layer is in doubt.

## Remedies for the two blocked locales

Any one of these clears a locale. Record the outcome here and update
`src/data/scriptureRights.ts`, then `npm run release-gate` must pass.

1. **Replace the text with a verified public-domain edition of the same
   language.** For French, eBible.org publishes the Louis Segond 1910 (public
   domain) and the historical Ostervald; the 1886 Ostervald also avoids the 1996
   layer while keeping the same textual tradition. For Portuguese, a dated,
   rights-documented Almeida edition must be sourced. Requires a new verified
   verbatim export and new bundled SHA-256 values.
2. **Obtain written permission** from Bearing Precious Seed / C. H. Boughman for
   French, and from the identified rights holder for Portuguese. File the
   permission and record it here.
3. **Remove the locale from the build** so no unverified Scripture ships. The UI
   translations can stay or go independently of the Scripture text.

Option 1 is recommended for French: the rights question is confined to the 1996
editorial layer, and an older PD Ostervald edition preserves the same tradition
without it.
