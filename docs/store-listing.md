# App Store & Google Play Listing Copy

ASO-optimized listing text for all 6 launch languages. Character limits noted are
the platform maximums (App Store / Google Play).

- **App name** (AS: 30 / GP: 30 chars) — include the top keyword.
- **Subtitle** (AS: 30) / **Short description** (GP: 80).
- **Keywords** (AS only, 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces needed).
- **Description** (AS: 4000 / GP: 4000).
- **Promo text** (AS: 170, editable without review) — use for seasonal hooks.

> Fill the developer name, support URL, and privacy URL in the store console.
> Screenshots come from the real app once art (A1–A17) lands.
