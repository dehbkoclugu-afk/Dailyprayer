# Scripture audio sources

Runtime audio is streamed over HTTPS and selected by exact edition name. Lumen does not bundle or silently substitute recordings.

| Japanese Freedom Bible 2026 | Disabled: exact recording edition and paid-product usage rights are unconfirmed | 0 enabled chapters | https://www.sermon-online.com/contents/25694 |

The Japanese host does not name the recording's text edition. Japanese audio remains outside the production source registry until both the exact edition match and paid-product usage rights are independently confirmed.

Audio remains streaming-only for the initial release. Offline downloads may be added later as an explicit per-book or per-chapter action, after chapter mapping is hardened and storage, integrity, progress, and deletion behavior are defined.
