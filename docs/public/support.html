How can we help?

For purchase, privacy, accessibility or app questions, email the developer. Include your app version and Android version, but never send passwords, payment-card details or private journal entries.

Purchases

Purchases are billed by Google Play. Lumen’s Restore Purchases action refreshes your Plus status; subscription cancellation and refund requests are handled through Google Play.

Delete local data

Use Profile → Delete all data. Uninstalling Lumen also removes content stored only on the device.
