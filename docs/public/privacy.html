Privacy Policy

Lumen (“the app”, “we”) is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • No account is required to use Lumen.
  • We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.
  • RevenueCat processes purchase history when access is purchased or restored.
  • Your journal, streaks, preferences and highlights stay on your device.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your first name and onboarding answers, prayer and reading progress, journal entries, highlighted verses, language, theme and reminder preferences are stored locally and are not transmitted to us. Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat, Inc. We receive anonymized subscription status so the app can unlock Plus content. We do not receive your full payment details. RevenueCat collects purchase history for app functionality and subscription analytics and encrypts it in transit. Apple, Google and RevenueCat handle payment information under their own privacy policies. Contact us to request deletion of the related RevenueCat customer record.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a server that sends push messages, and enabling a reminder does not send data to us.

Children

Lumen is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age in their country. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version shown inside the app.
