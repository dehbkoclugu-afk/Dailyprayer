Data Deletion

Last updated July 29, 2026

No account is required

Selaora: Daily Prayer & Bible does not create user accounts. Your journal entries, prayer requests, reading progress, highlights, streaks, name, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open your device settings.
  2. Choose Apps, then Selaora.
  3. Choose Storage and select Clear data, or uninstall Selaora.

This permanently removes Selaora's locally stored journal entries, prayer requests, progress, highlights, streaks, name, and preferences from that device. Because this data is not uploaded to CEY Studio, we cannot delete it remotely.

Request deletion of purchase-related data

Google Play and RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user identifier and purchase or subscription status to provide Selaora Plus. To request deletion of data that CEY Studio can control, email us using the button below.

Request data deletion

Include the platform you used and, if relevant, your Google Play order number. Do not send payment card information. We will respond and process eligible requests within 30 days.

Data that may be retained

Transaction records controlled by Google Play, the Apple App Store, or RevenueCat may be retained when required for financial reporting, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. Store-managed subscription records must be managed through the relevant app store.
