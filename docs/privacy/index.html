Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Selaora ("the app", "we") is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • No account is required to use Selaora.
  • We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.
  • Your journal entries, streaks, preferences, and highlights are stored **only on

your device**.

  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Journal security and backups

On Android and iOS, journal entries and prayer requests are encrypted on your device. The encryption key is kept in your device's secure store. Android app backup is disabled. There is no journal export or account recovery, so uninstalling the app, changing devices, or losing the key may permanently remove access to these entries. Other locally stored information is not covered by this additional journal encryption. There is no separate in-app biometric lock.

Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your first name (used only to greet you) and onboarding answers
  • Prayer/reading streaks and completion progress
  • Journal entries and prayer requests
  • Highlighted verses, language, theme, and reminder preferences

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Error reports

If error reporting is configured for a release, Selaora may send technical crash and error details to Sentry to diagnose failures. The app filters journal text, prayer requests, quiz answers, and user identity from reports. The information may include an app version, error category, and technical device details. Error reporting is inactive when no Sentry project is configured.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play through our payments provider, RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an anonymous app user ID to validate purchases, restore access, and understand subscription performance. This information is encrypted in transit. We do not receive your full payment details.

You may request deletion of the RevenueCat customer record associated with your installation. Open Profile, copy the support ID, and email it to dehbkoclugu@gmail.com. Apple or Google may retain store transaction records under their own legal obligations.

Downloads and network requests

Selaora works offline. It connects to the internet only to:

  • download optional Bible editions and language packs you choose, from our

public release page on GitHub (github.com). Like any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and basic device/network information; the request contains no personal data from the app. See GitHub's privacy statement.

  • check purchases through RevenueCat, which assigns an anonymous app user ID.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not run a server that sends you push messages, and enabling reminders does not send any data to us.

Children

Selaora is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the version in the app.
