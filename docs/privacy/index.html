Privacy Policy

Last updated July 28, 2026

The short version

  • No account is required to use Selaora.
  • We do not sell or share your personal data.
  • Your journal entries, streaks, preferences, and highlights stay on your device.
  • Selaora does not use third-party advertising or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The following information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your first name and onboarding answers
  • Prayer and reading streaks, plus completion progress
  • Journal entries and prayer requests
  • Highlighted verses, language, theme, and reminder preferences

Deleting the app removes this local data from your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store through RevenueCat. We receive only an anonymized subscription status, such as whether Selaora Plus is active, so the app can unlock content. We do not receive your full payment details.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not operate a server that sends push notifications, and enabling reminders does not send your data to us.

Children

Selaora is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its revision date.
